Our Story

From Marseille to Poitiers and back to Marseille with a final arrival in Lille – not to mention all the fun stopovers – from Maranello to Bologna to Pisa to Mallorca to Taormina to Aruba, not to mention Thomasville, Richmond, Quinton, Raleigh, Pigeon Forge and Dollywood, life has definitely been more fun together.


It’s been (well) over ten years that we’ve been traveling together and it’s definitely true that having someone to walk with improves life. 2025 was a very big year, we bought an apartment, got engaged, moved and planned this event together…


And somehow survived it all.


Now we want to celebrate all of this together, in Lille, this 16 May 2026.

We can’t wait to see all of you and to bask in the luxury of being together for a full day of celebrating and adventure.


Thank you to our friends and family for supporting us on this wonderful journey.


We can't wait to share our special day with you!
